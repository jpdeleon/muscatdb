MuSCAT + LCOGT Data Analysis & Scheduling Pipeline

Welcome to MuSCAT-db, the central astronomical data platform for target database, automated multi-color exoplanet photometry with prose2, transit fitting with timer, ephemeris and transit timing variation (TTV) analysis with harmonic, LCO observation window scheduling, and field-of-view optimization. For details, see the pipeline guide.

Transit Fitting

Ephemeris & TTV
Transit Timing Variation modeling and ephemeris calculation with harmonic.

About the Team

First light milestones, telescope configurations, and optical logos of the global MuSCAT multi-band imager series.

MuSCAT (2014)

First Light: 2014 at Okayama Astrophysical Observatory (OAO 1.88m, Japan). 3-band simultaneous optical imager.

MuSCAT2 (2017)

First Light: 2017 at Telescopio Carlos Sánchez (TCS 1.52m, Teide Observatory, Spain). 4-band optical imager.

MuSCAT3 (2020)

First Light: 2020 at Faulkes Telescope North (FTN 2.0m, Haleakalā, Hawaii). 4-band optical imager on LCOGT.

MuSCAT4 (2023)

First Light: 2023 at Faulkes Telescope South (FTS 2.0m, Siding Spring, Australia). 4-band optical imager on LCOGT.
